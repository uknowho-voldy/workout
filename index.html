Day A — Lower body
Tuesday · InShape Fitness · ~50 min
Hit 3×8 on squats → add 5 lbs next Tuesday. Log every set in Tracker.
Exercises
Primary lift
Barbell back squat
3 × 5–8 reps · 2 min rest
Setup
Bar on upper traps, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out
Descent
Sit back and down, chest up, knees track over toes, aim for parallel
Drive
Push floor away, drive hips forward, squeeze glutes at top
Compound
Romanian deadlift
3 × 8–10 reps · 2 min rest
Hinge
Push hips back, bar close to legs, feel hamstring stretch
Return
Drive hips forward, squeeze glutes at top
IT band note
Any knee discomfort → reduce weight, focus on hip hinge
Compound
Leg press
3 × 10–12 reps · 90s rest
Feet
Shoulder-width mid-platform · lower = more quad, higher = more glute
Range
Lower to ~90 degrees · don't let lower back lift off pad
Accessory
Walking lunges
2 × 12 each leg · 90s rest · bodyweight or light dumbbells
Core
Plank
3 × 30–45 seconds · 90s rest · Adonis belt goal
Core
Cable crunches / Ab wheel rollouts
3 × 10–12 reps · 90s rest · elbows to knees, abs do the work
Core
Hanging knee raises / Leg raises
3 × 12–15 reps · 90s rest
Day B — Upper body
Thursday · InShape Fitness · ~50 min
Hit 3×8 on bench → add 5 lbs next Thursday. Log every set in Tracker.
Exercises
Primary lift
Barbell bench press
3 × 5–8 reps · 2 min rest
Setup
Shoulder blades pinched back and down, slight arch, feet flat
Descent
Bar to lower chest, elbows at ~45–75 degrees, not flared wide
Compound
Barbell bent-over row
3 × 6–8 reps · 2 min rest
Position
Hinge to ~45 degrees, core braced
Pull
Drive elbows back, squeeze shoulder blades, bar to lower ribcage
Control
2 seconds down — no momentum
Compound
Overhead press
3 × 8–10 reps · 90s rest
Option
Barbell or dumbbells — dumbbells better if any shoulder impingement
Core
Brace abs — don't hyperextend lower back
Compound
Lat pulldown
3 × 10–12 reps · 90s rest
Focus
Think elbows to back pockets — not pulling with hands
Range
Full stretch at top — let shoulder blades rise. Pull to upper chest.
Accessory
DB curl + tricep pushdown (superset)
2 × 12 each · 90s rest · arms last, do not skip compounds
