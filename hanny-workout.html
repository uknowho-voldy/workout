First 2 weeks
Form over load
Weights stay deliberately light. The goal is learning the movement, not progressing the weight. If a set feels "easy," that's correct. Real progression starts week 3.
The progression rule
When to add weight
Hit the top of the rep range on all sets, with 1–2 reps still in the tank on the last set → add weight next session. Last rep should feel hard but doable. Not grinding. Not losing form.

Quad work
Leg press
3 × 10–12 · 90 sec rest
Feet
Shoulder-width, mid-platform · don't lock knees at the top
Range

Money lift
Hip thrust
3 × 10–12 · 60 sec rest
Setup
Upper back on bench, feet planted, barbell or DB across hips (use a pad)
Drive
Squeeze glutes hard at the top · body in a straight line from knees to shoulders
Why this matters
This is THE exercise for shape, knees, and future back health. Don't skip.
